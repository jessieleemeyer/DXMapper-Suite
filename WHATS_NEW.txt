WHAT'S NEW - DXMapper Suite (FM Tuner, RDS Monitor, DX Mapper)
==============================================================
Newest first. The Owner's Manual and Quick Start are updated with every
DXMapper Suite ZIP. Your settings folders are never touched by an update.

2026-10-13
----------
ADD STATION (STATIONS WITH NO RDS) - DX Mapper
- New "Add station..." button in the sidebar, for stations that don't
  send RDS. It reads the frequency the FM Tuner is on (no RDS needed;
  you can also type one) and lists every FCC station on it, closest
  first, with city, type (FM / LPFM / translator), power and distance.
- Mostly for IDing locals, but DX is in the list too. On the edge of a
  coverage area two stations can share a frequency, so nothing is picked
  for you: compare them and click Add on the one you hear.
- Type to narrow it down: call letters, a slogan ("Q102"), a city and
  state ("Springfield, IL"), a state ("ky" or "Kentucky") or a region
  ("central ky").
- A station you add by hand stays as you entered it. If RDS later
  decodes a different station on that frequency, it gets its own entry;
  if it's the same station, RDS only fills in blanks (like the PI).
- Now hearing works with no RDS: when the Tuner is on a frequency with
  no RDS, the Now hearing bar shows the station you logged there, marked
  "(no RDS)", with the map pulse, coverage outline and Follow tuner.
  Two or more logged on that frequency: it asks which one you're hearing
  (buttons) and never guesses; "change" picks another. Nothing logged
  there: it shows an "Add station..." button. RDS always takes over when
  it decodes. Nothing gets logged by itself.

LOGO PICKER - DX Mapper
- Station Details has a new "Change logo" button. It shows every logo
  the lookup can find online (Wikipedia, Facebook, the network's logo and
  the station's website) as small pictures. Click one to use it, or
  upload your own picture.
- Your pick replaces the station's logo straight away (in the RDS
  Monitor's settings\logos folder, so the monitor shows it too). After
  that nothing automatic changes it back - not the online lookup and not
  a logo decoded off HD. Only another pick changes it.

LOGO LOOKUP FIXES - DX Mapper / RDS Monitor
- NETWORK LOGO: a station with no logo of its own (e.g. a King of Kings
  Radio station) now gets its network's logo, from the network's
  Wikipedia page or the website on the station's Wikipedia page.
  Stations with their own logo (e.g. KISS FM stations) keep theirs.
- NO MORE GREY PERSON: Facebook's blank profile picture (the grey
  silhouette) is skipped. Some station pages also had a bit of Facebook
  code that was mistaken for a page called "2008"; that is fixed too.
- NO MORE BUILDING PHOTOS: when Wikipedia shows a studio, office or
  tower photo at the top of the page, the logo from the page's "Logo"
  line is used instead. Photos are skipped.
- Logos you already have are not re-checked. To fix one that is wrong
  (grey person, building photo), use Change logo.

MUSIC SEARCH BUTTONS - RDS Monitor
- Four small buttons under the song title and artist: YTM, SPOT, AMZ and
  APL. Each one searches the song on YouTube Music, Spotify, Amazon Music
  or Apple Music in your web browser.
- They light up by themselves in each service's colour (YouTube red,
  Spotify green, Amazon blue, Apple pink) while a song is showing (from
  RT+ or the RadioText), and go grey for promos, ads and station IDs.

GROUP SEQUENCE FINGERPRINT - RDS Monitor / DX Mapper
- A green console-style "SEQ>" line under the decode badges shows the
  last 24 RDS group types in the order they arrived (e.g. 0A 0A 2A 0A
  8A ...). Each station's encoder repeats its own pattern, so it works
  like a fingerprint for that transmitter.
- It is sent to DX Mapper with every log, saved in a new "Group Sequence"
  Log Book column (last column, also in the Excel export) and shown in
  Station Details. Each new catch updates it, unless you typed your own.

SISTER STATIONS & SAME MARKET - DX Mapper
- Station Details (map panel and Log Book card) has a new section just
  above Edit: Sister stations (from Wikipedia's "Sister stations" line,
  plus stations in your log with the same owner) and Same market (FM
  stations the FCC lists for the station's city, plus stations in your
  log from the same city and state). Uses your Log Book's City/State
  first, then the FCC's.
- Stations already in your log are links that open them; the rest are
  plain text. Lookups are saved for a month. Translators aren't listed.

NOW PLAYING ON THE MAP'S STATION CARD - DX Mapper
- When the station open in Station Details is the one the RDS Monitor
  is decoding live, a scrolling bar under the call letters shows the
  song (or the RadioText), the same as the Log Book's station card.
  It's hidden for stations that aren't on the air.

LOG BOOK SEARCH - DX Mapper
- "ky" finds Kentucky and "kentucky" finds KY (every US state and
  Canadian province). Classic view also finds a city and state typed
  together ("campbellsville ky"), even when those columns are hidden.

REMOTE WORDING - FM Tuner and guides
- The Remote panel (now "REMOTE (PHONE / TABLET / PC)"), its hint text,
  the Tuner README and every Quick Start copy now say it works on any
  phone or tablet (iPhone, iPad or Android) and any PC or Mac. It's a
  web page, so any browser works. Nothing about the remote itself changed.

TRANSLATOR LOGOS - DX Mapper / RDS Monitor
- A translator (e.g. W293BK relaying WTCO) keeps its own call letters
  but shows the logo of the station it relays (WTCO's). Download logo
  and Update all fetch the parent's logo instead of looking up the
  translator. A relayed HD2-HD8 channel uses that channel's logo,
  else the main station's.
- Works when the parent is an AM station (e.g. WTCO 1450 AM and its FM
  translator W294CO): the parent comes from the FCC record, WTFDA's
  "Relay of", the station's Wikipedia page or Fed by in Edit.
- An HD2-HD8 channel that simulcasts an AM station keeps its own call
  (WXXX-HD2) but uses the AM's logo, when Wikipedia's HD list says so
  (e.g. "HD2: Simulcast of WTCO 1450 AM") or when you type the AM's
  call in that HD channel's Fed by.
- The RDS Monitor no longer names the parent's PI after a translator
  logged in DX Mapper, so the main station keeps its own call letters.

HD CONTROLS - FM Tuner
- HD Seek Down / Up and the HD mode / channel controls are hidden unless
  the HD decoder add-on is installed (on the phone/tablet remote too).
  Only "Add HD decoder..." shows; the rest appears as soon as you add it.
- Region set to Europe, Japan or OIRT: there is no HD Radio there, so
  every HD control is hidden, including Add HD decoder. Switch Region
  back to Americas to see them.

LIFETIME COUNT - RDS Monitor
- Lifetime now counts the unique PI codes in your current DX Mapper log
  (the first one ticked in Logs...). HD2-HD8 share their station's PI, so
  they don't count extra. It goes up as you log and down if you delete.
- With no DX Mapper log it counts the old way (every PI ever decoded), and
  only then does Reset Lifetime Counter do anything.

GUIDE FIXES - Quick Start and Owner's Manual
- New RDS Monitor picture: TMC traffic is a ticker along the bottom.
- Automatic call letters from the PI code are US only. Outside the US,
  name the station once in DX Mapper (Edit) and the Monitor shows it.
- HackRF / SoapySDR support is marked EXPERIMENTAL until it is fixed.
- The remote is a web page: iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets and
  PCs, in any browser, nothing to install.
- Owner's Manual 5.4: station names come from DX Mapper's database; there
  is no PI override file to edit (stations.txt is only a background
  fallback the Monitor fills itself).

2026-10-12
----------
SEVERAL LOGS - DX Mapper
- Logs... (top left) makes, renames and deletes logs. Each log is its own
  file (settings\Logs\<name>.db, own Excel copy and backups), so deleting
  one never touches the others. Your current log is "Main" until renamed.
- Tick several logs to show together. The FIRST ticked gets new catches,
  edits and imports; the others are view only.
- A station in two logs shows once, with info from both and each log entry
  tagged with its log. Stations only in a view-only log keep a log badge.
- Copy to Current / -> Current copies an old station or catch over, with
  its whole HD family. The old log is never changed.
- "Also log new catches into the other ticked logs" (in Logs...) is OFF by
  default. Ticking it shows a warning popup; only "Turn it on" turns it on.

PUBLIC RELEASE CHECK
- scipy is now in requirements.txt and installed by Install All.bat. The
  FM Tuner needs it for sound; a new PC without it got "Audio unavailable".
  Listed in DEPENDENCIES.txt and the credits.
- Install All.bat now has Windows line endings.
- DEPENDENCIES.txt notes the map (Leaflet) loads from the internet.
- The Owner's Manual's multi-log example no longer names real towns.
- EULA copyright: (c) 2026 J. Meyer.
- The public ZIP now has a release number: "DXMapper Suite v2026.10.12.zip"
  (the build date; a second build the same day gets .2). The same number
  is in VERSION.txt inside it. The folder inside is still "DXMapper Suite",
  and your copy is still "DXMapper Suite Personal.zip".

FM TUNER - SAVE GAIN BUTTON
- "Remember gain per frequency" now has a Save gain button next to it.
  Gain is saved for the tuned frequency ONLY when you click Save gain;
  moving the slider, + / - or the mouse wheel no longer saves (it was
  saving in-between values). Saved gains still come back when you tune
  there again and on Start. Gains you saved before are kept.

ZIP AND FOLDER NAMES
- The ZIPs are now named after the project: "DXMapper Suite.zip" (public)
  and "DXMapper Suite Personal.zip" (personal copy, not for sharing). They
  replace Radio Public.zip and Radio.zip.
- Each ZIP holds one folder with the same name ("DXMapper Suite" or
  "DXMapper Suite Personal"), so you can tell the copies apart.
- The public ZIP's folder is "DXMapper Suite". Extract it to C:\ (or
  wherever you keep programs). Updating from a copy without that folder:
  rename your radio folder (e.g. C:\Radio) to "DXMapper Suite" first, then
  extract over it, so your settings stay with it.

EXCEL COPY AND IMPORT - DX Mapper
- DX Log.xlsx now has a bold warning on the top row of every sheet: it is a
  copy for viewing; edits typed in Excel are NOT read back. Make changes in
  DX Mapper, or bring an edited sheet in with Import...
- Import now only fills blanks. It never replaces call letters, PI, PS, PTY,
  slogan or other info your log already has.

RDS Monitor - station names from DX Mapper's log
- The Monitor reads call letters, city, coordinates and slogan straight
  from DX Mapper's logs (all of them, read only), so a fix made in DX
  Mapper shows within a second. stations.txt is only a fallback now.
- Removed: Open PI Codes File, Set Callsign for Current PI and Reload PI
  Overrides. Fix stations in DX Mapper instead.

ONE FOLDER - DXMapper Suite is now one program
- FM Tuner, RDS Monitor and DX Mapper are in one folder with ONE settings
  folder, one Install All, one INSTALL.txt and one requirements.txt.
- FM_Tuner.py, RDS Monitor.py and DX Mapper.py sit next to Start Radio for
  quick launch of just one part (no console window).
- First start copies your settings (and rtlsdr.dll) from the old FM-Tuner,
  RDS-Monitor and DX-Mapper folders. The old folders are not touched, so
  you can go back; delete them once everything works.
- rtlsdr.dll and libusb-1.0.dll now go in the main folder.

RDS Monitor - GPS (optional)
- Tools > GPS Location (USB GPS dongle)... finds the dongle by itself,
  shows the fix and sets Home or Away from it.
- "Keep the Away location updated from GPS while traveling": Away follows
  you (at most once a minute, after moving half a mile). DX Mapper follows.
- Not required: without a dongle you type Home/Away as before. Uses the
  pyserial add-on that Install All puts in.

FM Tuner - gain memory fixes
- Remembered gain now also comes back when you press Start.
- A gain change made right after tuning is saved to the new frequency,
  not the one you just left.
- Recall re-sends the saved gain to the dongle even if the slider already
  shows it.
DX Mapper - Log Book
- Modern view: Classic | Modern switch. Station cards with search,
  now-playing bar, Local / Extended Local / DX Catches / Show all tabs,
  logo backgrounds and PI PTY RT RT+ iTunes TMC lights.
- Station panel with the same sections as Station Details, Wikipedia
  summary, Edit info, Delete station and x on each Log history entry
  (both ask in a confirmation popup first).
- Follows the map; "Open station card" in Station Details jumps to the
  card; "Show on map" in the panel goes the other way.
- Call letters: regular stations and HD1 are just call letters (WAPD);
  HD2-HD8 show as WAPD-HD2 on their own card.
- Splatter: auto logs of a PI already logged on another frequency join
  that station (unless the FCC lists a translator there). Pressing Log in
  the RDS Monitor always logs where you're tuned.
- One entry per station: a station moved onto another entry's call and
  frequency is merged at once. Duplicates... merges doubles already there
  (same or other frequency) and saves a backup first.
DX Mapper - Station Details
- Only defined ODAs are listed; junk unregistered AIDs from damaged

- "Hide my location" checkbox for sharing screenshots.
RDS Monitor
- TMC traffic messages scroll in a ticker along the bottom of the window.
  (It still decodes every group, even without a 3A, for weak stations.)
FM Tuner
- "Remember gain per frequency" moved right under Manual/Automatic.
Guides
- Thumb drive / travel laptop instructions; WHATS_NEW.txt (this file).
