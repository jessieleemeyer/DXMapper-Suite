8. STANDARDS, TABLES AND FILE FORMATS THE CODE FOLLOWS
Written from these published standards and lists. Short tables from them
(PTY names, RT+ classes, ODA names, TMC event phrases and durations) are
reproduced for decoding only and remain the property of their publishers.
- RBDS: NRSC-4-B, National Radio Systems Committee (PI to call letters,
  U.S. PTY names)
- RDS: IEC 62106 / EN 50067, IEC and CENELEC
- RadioText Plus (RT+): RDS Forum specification
- Registered ODA list: RDS Forum and NRSC
- TMC: ALERT-C, ISO 14819-1 and ISO 14819-2 (a subset of event phrases)
- HD Radio: NRSC-5 (decoded only by the separate add-on above)
- RDS Spy .spy files and the ASCII "G" TCP format: RDS Spy by Jan Kolar
  and SDR# RDSOutput (Airspy), for compatibility only
